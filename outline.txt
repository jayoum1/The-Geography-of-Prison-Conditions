Formal outline + documentary

Thesis: The federal government should allocate funding to states for general prison operation costs beyond the existing funding for training, technology and rehabilitation programs.

*Important directional disclaimer: federal funding should be purposefully directed towards improving minimum living standards in prisons, and not to expanding imprisonment as past examples like the Crime Bill has taught us

For each of the prison condition categories, have 1 or two main example stories and states. Narrate the story, and for the representative state make a deeper dive, including:

More data graphs, including scatterplots comparing distinct statistics (e.g. incarceration rate vs. prison occupancy)
Interesting statistics about the state (e.g. state with lowest officer salary stands out for poor prisoner treatment)
Visually, don’t only mention the statistic but bring back a “mini-map” where only that state is highlighted and the statistic is shown
A central argument that the state represents within the broader narrative of funding and prison conditions (e.g. less salary = poor treatment)
If possible, have the state and story be aligned
